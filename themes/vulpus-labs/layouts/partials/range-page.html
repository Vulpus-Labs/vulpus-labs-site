Installing

Unzip the download and copy the .clap file into your CLAP folder, or the .vst3 into your VST3 folder, then rescan plugins in your DAW:

macOS     CLAP  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/
          VST3  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
Windows   CLAP  C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP\
          VST3  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
Linux     CLAP  ~/.clap/
          VST3  ~/.vst3/

The macOS builds are signed and notarized by Apple. The Windows and Linux builds are not signed: if Windows blocks a plugin, right-click the downloaded zip, choose Properties, tick Unblock and unzip it again. Checksums are on each effect's page.
