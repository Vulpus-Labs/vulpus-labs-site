## Bugs and feature requests

Please raise these as issues on the repository for the product, so they stay
visible and searchable:

- [vxn-1](https://github.com/Vulpus-Labs/vxn-1/issues) — VXN1b and VXN2 both
  live in this repository
- [patches](https://github.com/Vulpus-Labs/patches/issues) — the Patches engine,
  DSL and player
- [patches-bundles](https://github.com/Vulpus-Labs/patches-bundles/issues) —
  add-on module bundles
- [vulpus-labs-vm](https://github.com/Vulpus-Labs/vulpus-labs-vm/issues) —
  Voltage Modular modules

Include your OS, DAW and plugin version — the version is shown on each product
page and in the plugin's own about panel.

## Voltage Modular modules

Vulpus Labs modules for Voltage Modular are sold through the
[Cherry Audio store](https://store.cherryaudio.com/manufacturers/vulpus-labs).
Store, purchase and licensing issues go to Cherry Audio; anything about how a
module *behaves* comes to me.
