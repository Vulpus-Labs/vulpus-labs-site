The VXN range — synthesizers as CLAP plugins for macOS and Windows, each
playable in the browser before you buy anything — plus PolyKritter, and
Patches, a modular audio engine driven by a plain-text patch language.

Everything here is built in Rust. vxn-1c and vxn-2b are coming soon to KVR
Marketplace; their free predecessors, VXN1b and VXN2, are still downloadable
from their pages.
