The manual for [Glow-Up](/products/glow-up/) covers:

- a quick start: what you hear at the defaults, and what to try first;
- what Glow-Up does, and the boosts' ratios, with graphs of what each lifts;
- FOCUS, from fixed resonances to exact tracking, and the three grids;
- RESONANCE, WIDTH, SPEED, TONE and THRESHOLD, each shown at three or four settings;
- the screen, output and mode, starting points and tips;
- a reference of every control's range and default.
