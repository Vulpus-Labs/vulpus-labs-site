The manual for the [PolyKritter](/products/polykritter/) plugin and web instrument covers:

- every control, section by section, with a labelled view of each part of the faceplate;
- VCO 1's waves and scales, VCO 2's tuning, the effect section and the noise;
- the filter's frequency responses, the envelope, the hertz-domain vibrato and Bender, Repeat
  and glide, measured from the plugin's own code;
- voices, Solo and Legato, and stacks of up to sixteen voices a note;
- the modulation matrix: every source, destination and curve;
- the chorus, delay and reverb pages, the output stage and the scope;
- presets, RAND (a new random sound), and six worked patches from the factory bank, each with what it changes
  from Init;
- a reference table of every range and time constant, and troubleshooting.

The Voltage Modular module has [its own manual](/manuals/polykritter-voltage/).
