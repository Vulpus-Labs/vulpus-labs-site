The manual for [vxn-1c](/products/vxn-1c/) covers:

- every control, panel by panel, with a labelled view of each part of the faceplate;
- the oscillators' waves, cross modulation (sync, FM and ring), and aliasing at each QUALITY;
- the ladder filter's modes, slopes, resonance and drive, and the layer high-pass, as
  frequency responses;
- envelope and LFO shapes, stacking, detune and spread, Poly and Solo;
- the modulation matrix: every source, destination, curve and depth law;
- the layers, the split and LINK, the arpeggiator, and the effects;
- presets, RANDOM, and seven worked patches from the factory bank, each with what it changes
  from Init;
- a reference table of every range and time constant, and troubleshooting.
