The manual covers:

- every control and jack, section by section, with a labelled view of each part of the panel;
- the controls that start with no effect, and where;
- the filter's frequency responses, including resonance and its level dependence;
- envelope shapes, Repeat's clock and its restart, and the hertz-domain vibrato, Bender and
  portamento;
- VCO 1's harmonic spectra, VCO 2's tuning modes, the noise spectrum and aliasing across the
  keyboard;
- a reference table of every range and time constant.
