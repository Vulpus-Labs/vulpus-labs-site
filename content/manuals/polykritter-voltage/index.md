The manual covers:

- every control, section by section, with a labelled view of each part of the panel;
- the patchbay: what each CV input does and what each output carries, with ranges;
- the filter's frequency responses, including resonance and its level dependence;
- envelope shapes, Repeat's clock and phrasing, and the hertz-domain vibrato, Bender and
  portamento;
- VCO 1's harmonic spectra, VCO 2's tuning modes and the noise spectrum;
- ECO against HI: the top-octave response and aliasing across the keyboard;
- a reference table of every range and time constant.
