The manual for [vxn-2b](/products/vxn-2b/) covers:

- every control, plate by plate, with a labelled view of each part of the faceplate;
- the thirty-two algorithms, feedback, and each operator's tuning, velocity and level;
- operator envelopes and key scaling, as drawn and edited on the operator's LCD, with their
  times and curves measured;
- the pitch EG and the mod env, voices and stacks, and the two LFOs;
- the modulation matrix: every source, destination and curve, and the routes it flags;
- the filter and the high-pass, the effects, and QUALITY's oversampling and aliasing;
- presets, RANDOM, and seven worked patches from the factory bank, each with what it changes
  from Init;
- a reference table of every range and time constant, and troubleshooting.
