The manual covers:

- every control and jack, row by row, with a labelled view of each part of the panel;
- how the shift works: partials found, moved whole and kept in phase from frame to frame;
- the spectral envelope: PRESERVE, FORMANT and DETAIL, with a sung vowel shifted, held and moved;
- the three windows, their delay and frequency resolution, and what the method does to attacks,
  low notes and noise;
- TRANSIENT, measured on an impulse and a burst of noise;
- feedback, its returns rising in octaves, and the three freeze modes, with a frozen noise band in
  HOLD and in BLUR;
- a reference table of every range and law.
